# Maintain inventory quantities through immutable stock transactions

Inventory on-hand balances are determined by immutable stock-in, stock-out, and counted-balance adjustment transactions; corrections use reversals and reposting rather than editing history. Accepting recovered material posts a stock-in linked to its demolition project and inventory item. This keeps current quantities traceable to recorded movements, unlike direct quantity edits, while requiring opening balances and corrections to be represented explicitly in the ledger.
