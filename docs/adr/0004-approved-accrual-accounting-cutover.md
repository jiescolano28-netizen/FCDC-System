# Approved accrual accounting cutover

FCDC's accounting design targets complete accrual books for one entity rather than presenting a POS/operational subset as complete company financial statements. Begin from an accountant-approved cutover, chart/mappings and reconciled opening account, supplier and valued-stock schedules instead of inventing postings from incomplete pre-cutover history; midyear YTD/year results additionally require clearly identified approved pre-cutover income/expense summaries, while unsupported historical ranges remain unavailable. Non-POS activity and applicable purchase VAT use authorized journals, AR remains GL-only, and the separately approved POS-only tax reports do not become a determination of final company VAT liability.

This is a confirmed design decision, not evidence of implementation. The full contract and activation prerequisites are in [the accounting workflow design](../accounting-workflow-design.md).
