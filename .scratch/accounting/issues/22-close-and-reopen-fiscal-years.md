# 22: Close fiscal years and preserve reported operating income

**What to build:** An accountant can transfer a reconciled January–December year into retained earnings exactly once and safely undo/reclose that transfer when the authorized period chain reopens.

**Blocked by:** 21: Close and dependency-check reopening of accounting months.

**Status:** complete

- [x] Explicitly authorize a once-only linked fiscal closing entry transferring revenue/expense account balances to approved retained earnings, including reviewed pre-cutover YTD opening amounts when applicable.
- [x] Require balanced books, matching controlled schedules, reconciled equation and eligible monthly period states; reject duplicate, unauthorized or unapproved-account closing.
- [x] Income Statement and its eligible YTD/year result exclude closing entries and their reversals; Trial Balance and Balance Sheet include them, with no duplicated unclosed earnings.
- [x] Reopening the fiscal year/dependent periods reverses the affected linked closing entry before corrections, preserves original closing history, and requires a new close after reconciliation.
- [x] Display fiscal-close status, journal/reversal links and reasons in period administration and existing financial detail surfaces.
- [x] Demonstrate closing a profitable year once, unchanged annual operating income, transferred retained earnings and zero duplicated current earnings; then reopen/correct/reclose and cover duplicate rejection.
