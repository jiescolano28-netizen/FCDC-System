# 07: Persisted Operational Dashboard and Sales Reports

**What to build:** Business users can inspect dashboard sales, inventory, low-stock views, and weekly, monthly, or yearly sales reports based on persisted application records rather than illustrative browser activity.

**Blocked by:** 03: Persisted Inventory Management and Selling Prices; 04: Paid Cash or Card Checkout.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Dashboard sales and inventory summaries derive from persisted application records.
- [ ] Low-stock views reflect persisted quantities and reorder levels.
- [ ] Weekly, monthly, and yearly sales views derive from persisted sale records.
- [ ] Production operational views do not substitute browser fixture values for persisted activity.
- [ ] Feature coverage verifies report and dashboard results against recorded inventory and sales.
