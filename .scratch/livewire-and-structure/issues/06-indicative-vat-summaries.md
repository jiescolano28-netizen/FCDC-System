# 06: Indicative VAT Summaries

**What to build:** Business users can review internal VAT transaction details and monthly or quarterly summaries derived from persisted sales, with clear notices that the figures are not statutory returns and are not filed with a tax authority.

**Blocked by:** 04: Paid Cash or Card Checkout; 05: Persistent Company Settings.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] VAT transaction details and period summaries derive from persisted sales and the saved indicative tax rate, not browser fixtures.
- [ ] Users can filter internal tax reports by period and print them.
- [ ] VAT is clearly labeled indicative, and screens state that they are not statutory returns and do not file with a tax authority.
- [ ] Feature coverage verifies calculated values and monthly/quarterly totals from actual sales.
