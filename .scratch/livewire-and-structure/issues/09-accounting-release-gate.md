# 09: Withhold Accounting and Full Application Release

**What to build:** The application does not present invented accounting behavior, and the requested full release remains explicitly gated until approved accounting rules are supplied and implemented.

**Blocked by:** 01: Livewire Foundation and Public Homepage; 02: Employee Authentication and Protected Application Shell; 03: Persisted Inventory Management and Selling Prices; 04: Paid Cash or Card Checkout; 05: Persistent Company Settings; 06: Indicative VAT Summaries; 07: Persisted Operational Dashboard and Sales Reports; 08: Isolated, Visibly Marked Demo Environment.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Accounting screens, routes, and navigation are withheld; no placeholder accounting behavior is exposed.
- [ ] No chart of accounts, automated postings, payable/disbursement lifecycle, ledger, trial balance, or financial statements are invented.
- [ ] Full application release remains blocked until an approved chart of accounts, posting rules, payable and disbursement rules, and report definitions are supplied and implemented.
- [ ] The withheld-accounting state and release prerequisite are clearly communicated to maintainers and users as appropriate.
