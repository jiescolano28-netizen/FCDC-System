# 05: Persistent Company Settings

**What to build:** Employees can update company details, indicative tax rate, and currency, and those choices persist for later reports and receipts.

**Blocked by:** 02: Employee Authentication and Protected Application Shell.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Authorized application users can save company name, address, phone, indicative tax rate, and currency.
- [ ] The default indicative tax rate is 12%, is clearly labeled, and can be configured.
- [ ] Saved settings are shown on a subsequent request and are used by relevant receipt and report views.
- [ ] Feature coverage verifies that settings persist across requests.
